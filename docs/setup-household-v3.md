# Household: simpler shopping

Five minutes. Nothing to change in the hub or the sheets; new columns are added by themselves.

## 1. Upload the files

Unzip `life-apps-household-v3.zip`, then on **github.com/ziyadapps/life-apps**: **Add file**, **Upload files**, drag everything inside the **life-apps** folder, **Commit changes**. Wait for the green tick, then about 10 minutes.

Open **Household** twice on each phone (close it in between) so the new version takes over. Do the same once for Budget and Today: a shared file changed, so every app refreshes.

## 2. Check the Arabic wording

Each phone is spoken to as whoever holds it. A phone whose key opens Budget gets the masculine wording; the others get the feminine. To change it on a phone: **Lists and settings**, with the app in Arabic, **التطبيق يخاطب في هذا الجوال**.

## How shopping works now

The list is the shopping screen.

1. **Start shopping** (or just tick the first item). Tap the store you are at; it is optional, but with it the app compares prices for that store.
2. **Tick** what you take. It goes into **In the basket** at the bottom. Tick it again to put it back on the list.
3. **Want the price?** Tap the item instead of ticking it:
   - **Seen before**: what it cost at each store, cheapest first.
   - Set the quantity with − and +.
   - Type the price and choose **each** or **for all**: 2 tins at 99.50 each, or 199 for both, come out the same (2 × 99.50 = 199.00).
   - As you type it says, for example, **14.50 more than at Danube (85.00)**, or how it compares with last time at this store.
   - **On offer** for a shelf offer. **Into the basket** to take it. **Not here** if the store does not have it.
4. **Checkout** (the bar at the bottom shows the basket's total). Check the store, type the **receipt total**, choose the envelope, **Save**.
   - What the items add up to is shown above, to check the receipt against.
   - A receipt lower than the items is a **checkout discount** (like 30% off the second tin). The shelf prices stay as they were, so the price history stays true. When more than one item has a price, you can say which item it was on.
   - Items without a price are fine: the receipt total is what goes to Budget.
5. Bought items **leave the list by themselves**. Nothing asks for a price or a store afterwards.

**Stop shopping** (at the bottom of the list, or on Checkout) puts the basket back on the list.

## Editing and deleting items

- On a list: tap the item, **Edit item**.
- On **Add**: every result has **Edit**: rename it, change its section, or **Delete this item and its prices** (tap twice). A catalogue item is hidden instead, since the catalogue is part of the app.
- **Often bought** only shows items bought on two different days, not items you just added to a list.

## Your old items

Items ticked with the old version ("Bought") are no longer shown on the lists. Their prices are kept in the history.
