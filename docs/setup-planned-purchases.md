# Budget update: planned purchases, and subscriptions only where they belong

About 5 minutes. The hub script does not change.

## 1. Upload

Unzip `life-apps-budget-update.zip`, then on **github.com/ziyadapps/life-apps**: **Add file**, **Upload files**, drag everything inside the **life-apps** folder, **Commit changes**. Wait for the green tick, then about 10 minutes. Open **Budget** twice (close it in between).

## 2. The envelope

If you have not made it yet: **More**, **Envelopes and accounts**, **Add envelope**:

| Field | Type |
|---|---|
| Name | Planned purchases (or what you like) |
| Kind | Fund (what is left builds up) |
| Kept in | Rajhi Savings |
| Standard month amount | What goes in each payday |

Then move what is already saved for it into the envelope (**Move money**).

## 3. Start the list

Open the envelope, **Settings**, **Start a planned purchases list**, then **Add something** for each thing: name, price, and a "wanted by" date if it has one.

## How it works

- The list is in your order. **▲ ▼** move a thing up or down.
- What is saved now pays for the top of the list. Each thing shows **Ready now**, or the payday it becomes affordable at the envelope's monthly amount ("About Feb 2027, in 3 paydays").
- A thing with a "wanted by" date that will not be ready in time shows in red, with what a month it would need.
- The top of the list shows what is saved, what goes in each month, the whole list, and when everything is bought.
- **Bought it**: open the thing, type what you paid, **Mark as bought**. The payment comes out of the envelope and it moves to **Bought**, where **Undo** takes it back.
- The envelope's page shows **Planned purchases** and the next thing on the list.

## Also in this update

**Subscriptions** shows on an envelope only when it has some. To start a subscriptions list on another envelope, use its **Settings**.
