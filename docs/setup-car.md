# Car

About 15 minutes. The hub script does not change.

---

## 1. Upload the files

Unzip `life-apps-car.zip`, then on **github.com/ziyadapps/life-apps**: **Add file**, **Upload files**, drag everything inside the **life-apps** folder, **Commit changes**. Wait for the green tick, then about 10 minutes.

Open **Budget** and **Today** twice each (close them in between), so they pick up the update.

## 2. Give the car its own sheet

In the **Life apps hub** sheet: **Life apps** menu, **Add an app...**, type `car`, OK. It makes **Life apps: car** in your Drive, backed up every night like the others.

Your iPhone and Laptop keys open every app, so no new key is needed.

## 3. The app on your iPhone

In Safari open `ziyadapps.github.io/life-apps/car/`, **Share**, **Add to Home Screen**, open **Car** from the home screen and unlock with your **iPhone** key.

## 4. The car's details

**Car details** (or **More**, **Settings**):

| Field | What to type |
|---|---|
| Name | What you call the car |
| Received | The day you got it. Months-based service items count from here |
| Fuel price a litre | Leave empty for 2.18 (91 octane) |
| Schedule | Normal + dust checks (see below) |
| A regular service | Leave empty until your first paid service, or the dealer's price if you know it |
| Extra parts | Leave empty for now; add a price when you get a quote |
| Financing | Monthly amount, number of instalments, first and last payment dates, the final (balloon) payment |
| Dates | Insurance end, registration expiry, first periodic inspection, warranty end if you know it |

**Save settings**.

## 5. The first fill-up and today's km

1. **+** (the middle button): the odometer at that fill-up, the date, the cost. The litres fill in from the price; correct them if the pump showed something else. **Filled to the top** stays on. **Log fill-up**.
2. Tap the big km figure on the Car screen, type today's odometer, **Save reading**.

From the second reading on, the app knows your km a day, and every service gets a date.

## 6. Budget: which envelopes are the car's

In **Budget**: **More**, **Envelopes and accounts**, tap the envelope, **Car app**:

- Your fuel envelope: **Fuel: fill-ups post here**.
- Your car envelope: **Maintenance: services post here**.

**Save envelope** each time.

---

## How it works

### Fill-ups

Each one is the odometer, the cost and the litres (worked out for you). From one full tank to the next, the app shows your real km a litre and cost a km. A fill-up that was not to the top is added into the next full one.

- At a station that charges more, change **Price a litre** on that fill-up. The next one starts at 2.18 again.
- Logged here first: it posts to the Fuel envelope next time Budget opens. When the bank's message comes, Budget sees the same amount on the same day and counts it once.
- The bank's message came first: Budget logs it, and Car lists it under **Fuel**, **From the bank**, with a banner on the Car screen. Tap it, add the odometer, save. Nothing is logged twice.
- Changing or deleting a fill-up changes or removes its payment in Budget.

### Services

The plan is the owner's manual for the Corolla with the M20A-FKS engine. **More**, **Maintenance plan** shows every item, when it was last done and when it is next due, by km or by date, whichever comes first.

- **Normal**: the manual's regular table.
- **Normal + dust checks**: the regular table, plus air filter checks every 2,500 km, the A/C filter every 15,000 km and brake checks every 5,000 km. The quick checks have a **Done** button; the next service does them anyway when it comes first.
- **Dusty**: the manual's dusty roads table: oil every 5,000 km or 6 months, plus the dust checks.

**Service** lists the services coming up in the next year, what each covers and what it should cost. When something falls due, anything else due before about halfway to the next service is done at the same visit.

**Log a service** comes ticked with what was due. Untick what they did not do, type the odometer and the cost (0 if free), save. A paid service posts to the Maintenance envelope. The price of a plain service (oil, filter, checks) is learned from the last one you logged.

### What to set aside

The Car screen shows, for next month:

- **Fuel**: your km a day, times the days in the month, at your km a litre and the fuel price. Until two full tanks show your real km a litre, it uses the sticker's figure.
- **Maintenance, saved monthly**: the next 12 months of services spread evenly, so the bigger ones are already saved when they come.

In Budget, **Plan** shows the same figures under the Fuel and Maintenance envelopes: **Car suggests ... Use it**.

### Reminders

On the Car screen and in Today: a service within two weeks or 1,000 km, a quick check that is due, insurance, registration and inspection dates as they come close, the final payment six months ahead, and any fill-up from the bank that needs its km.
